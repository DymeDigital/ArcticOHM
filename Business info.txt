Arctic Ohm — Company Description
Arctic Ohm is a trusted air-conditioning, refrigeration, and appliance repair centre located at 294 Titren Road, Seaview. We are proud stockists of leading brands in air-conditioning and refrigeration parts, as well as suppliers of new units for residential and commercial applications.

Our experienced team provides reliable appliance repair and technical services, helping customers keep their air-conditioning, refrigeration, and household appliances operating efficiently. Whether you need replacement parts, a new air-conditioning or refrigeration unit, or professional appliance repairs, Arctic Ohm is committed to providing quality products, dependable service, and practical solutions.
